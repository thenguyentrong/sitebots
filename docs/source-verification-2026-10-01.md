# First source-verification batch — 2026-10-01

## Scope and method

This is a bounded research batch for the broader Sitebots scope: physical work across industries and robot types. It covers Unitree G1 and G1 EDU, Boston Dynamics Spot, ANYbotics ANYmal, and MiR250. It does **not** constitute an audit of every catalogue entry or a production-data import.

Checked on **2026-10-01** using official manufacturer pages and German/EU seller pages through the web research tool. `Directly read` below means the tool returned the page body; it is not proof of an uncached origin-server response or of current stock. `Indexed only` means the search index supplied content that could not be confirmed by opening the page. No repository crawler was run for this batch. Future automated refresh/import must use `scripts/scrape/_lib/fetch.ts` and the existing source/attribution rules.

Manufacturer-supported specifications, seller-published purchase conditions, and manufacturer-published customer stories are different evidence classes. None establishes the success rate of a new customer's task. No supplier inquiries were sent. Existing curated records, purchasing contacts, lifecycle reviews, and the database were left unchanged.

### Conflicts to retain, not average away

1. **G1: 2 kg / 3 kg / 6 kg.** The reviewed official comparison supports base ~2 kg and EDU ~3 kg, under posture-dependent arm limits. It does not establish the catalogue's reported 6 kg bilateral continuous rating. Keep that third-party claim separate until its exact configuration and test conditions are sourced. Do not double a single-arm figure. [Official comparison](https://www.unitree.com/g1/)
2. **G1 variant and height inconsistency in seller copy.** Delta's introduction says roughly 132 cm, but its specification table says 1,270 mm. Its EDU-U2 table gives 29 DOF; RBTX's selected U2 page gives 43 DOF. These can reflect incompatible option packages or stale descriptions. Match manufacturer part numbers and hands before importing. [Delta listing](https://www.deltarobots.de/roboter/unitree-g1/), [RBTX U2 listing, indexed only](https://rbtx.com/en-US/components/humanoid/unitree-g1-edu-humanoid-robot-advanced-version-u2)
3. **Spot official sources disagree.** The directly read product page gives 14 kg mounted payload. An indexed official support table describes 20.43 kg maximum recommended payload with placement/stability caveats, and ~60 min runtime when powering payloads. Directly opening that support URL returned 404. Treat the larger limit as an unresolved, indexed-official claim. It is not eligible to replace the live record without matching hardware/manual evidence. [Product page](https://bostondynamics.com/products/spot/), [support table requiring confirmation](https://support.bostondynamics.com/articles/Knowledge/Spot-Specifications-49916)
4. **Spot base versus arm.** The official arm datasheet separately gives up to 11 kg peak lift and 5 kg continuous lift at 0.5 m extension, measured at 22°C. These are arm/configuration figures, not the base payload value. The datasheet was found in indexed official PDF content; refresh/read the correct current version before importing. [Arm datasheet](https://bostondynamics.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/spot-arm.pdf)
5. **ANYmal mission versus travel range.** The indexed technical PDF distinguishes up to 4 km full-charge travel from up to 2 km typical inspection, depending on payload and inspection points. The product page's 2 km should not be silently combined with a four-kilometre transport claim. [Technical PDF, indexed](https://www.anybotics.com/anymal-technical-specifications.pdf)
6. **MiR250 uptime and passage width.** Marketing describes up to 20 h/day operation and 80 cm access; the technical table distinguishes loaded/unloaded runtime and gives a default 1,500 mm doorway. The 800 mm doorway requires a minimized footprint and muted protective fields. Those conditions must travel with the value; they are not an unconditional recommendation. [Overview, indexed](https://mobile-industrial-robots.com/products/robots/mir250), [technical table, directly read](https://mobile-industrial-robots.com/products/robots/mir250/specifications)
7. **MiR250 platform versus application.** The indexed Shelf Carrier 250 page advertises a separate 300 kg / 10 h / 1.2 m/s application specification, while the base robot is 250 kg. Resolve application version and payload accounting before presenting a combined system. SPIE separately lists 500 kg towing; this is a seller claim for a towing configuration, not a base carrying capacity. [Shelf Carrier, indexed](https://mobile-industrial-robots.com/products/applications/mir-shelf-carrier-250), [SPIE, directly read](https://www.spie-automation.com/leistungen/robotik/transportroboter/)

## Evidence fields to add before wider refresh

- **Identity:** manufacturer, family, generation, exact SKU, hardware revision, firmware/software version, installed hands/arm/sensors, top module, battery and region.
- **A specification assertion:** field, raw value, normalized value/unit, semantic type (mounted/arm/tow; peak/continuous; one arm/two arms), load centre/reach/posture, environment, test procedure, exclusions, source URL/title/type/tier, publisher, publication/revision date, observed date and retrieval mode.
- **Verification state:** manufacturer-supported, reported, unknown, conflicting, superseded; reviewer; rationale; competing assertions; original excerpt with small quotation limits; next review date. Preserve `null` for missing numbers.
- **Availability:** announced, prototype, research kit, commercially orderable or discontinued; market; exact configuration; seller; gross/net currency price; shipping/tax basis; buyer restrictions; lead-time text; stock text; checked date. Keep lifecycle separate from task readiness.
- **Contacts:** public business role/email/phone/form, source, country/served markets, sales versus service versus integration, authorization evidence and validity date, last checked, warranty/SLA scope when published.
- **A task deployment:** task/environment/object; robot configuration; tool/payload/software; operator mode (teleoperated, supervised autonomy, autonomous); deployment stage; hours/cycles; attempts/successes/interventions with denominators; failure conditions; evidence publisher and independence; date; reproducibility; installation requirements; local-service gaps.
- **Visible confidence:** show evidence coverage alongside a task-fit verdict. Do not turn absent evidence into zero capability, demonstrations into production readiness, or a technical maximum into expected throughput.
