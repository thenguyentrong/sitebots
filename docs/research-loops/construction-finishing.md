## Decisions that prevent misleading matches

- The NOVA-S records represent different hardware configurations, not two names for the same complete machine. Pillar and drive-unit masses stay separate. Spray-rate maxima do not represent accepted area per paid shift.
- ConBotics' current 2.0 homepage conflicts with legacy product content. Unresolved exact-revision height and mass stay null. Placeholder testimonials were excluded.
- EG7+ demonstration figures are not transferred to EG7. European fleet activity does not prove German delivery or an exact-model deployment.
- JLG currently hosts the Canvas product. The current and launch footprints differ; historic family projects do not establish 1200CX routine operation. US finishing levels are not treated as German Q classifications.
- Standard Brokk 110/300 remain separate from the newer plus variants. Reach depends on the reference hammer, while the Brokk 300 customer evidence concerns a crusher.
- The fresh German DXR 145 listing agrees with the manufacturer's current weight. An older indexed alternate path differs; the current complete-product weight definition is retained. The seller's stock and lead-time statements remain explicitly seller claims.
- Painting links cover open-surface application, with masking, edges and material handling outside the demonstrated scope. Drywall links do not establish automatic taping or complete finish acceptance. Demolition matches require a human operator and a specified attachment; no asbestos or waste-sorting match was added.
