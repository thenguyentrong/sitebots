## Figure 02: historical evidence

Figure 02 loaded sheet-metal parts into fixtures at BMW Spartanburg; other industrial robots performed welding. BMW calls the completed deployment a pilot and reports more than 90,000 components and about 1,250 operating hours. Figure announced the start of fleet retirement in November 2025. These historical results are not Figure 03 results and do not establish current Figure 02 orderability. [Figure deployment retrospective](https://www.figure.ai/news/production-at-bmw), [BMW customer retrospective](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/united-kingdom/article/detail/T0455870EN_GB/bmw-group-to-deploy-humanoid-robots-in-production-in-germany-for-the-first-time).

## Walker S2: industrial configuration only

The industrial product graphic directly supports 176 cm height, 70 kg mass, 52 body DoF, fourth-generation 11-DoF hands, 7.5 kg single-hand grasp and 2 m/s walking speed. Product text separately describes a 15 kg handling load, 0–1.8 m vertical workspace, ±162° waist rotation and a battery swap within three minutes. Those quantities have separate semantics; none becomes a guaranteed loaded runtime or task throughput. Pitch endpoints of −35° to +90° explain the narrative’s 125° total span. [Industrial product page](https://www.ubtrobot.com/en/humanoid/products/walker-s2), [product specification graphic](https://owebsite-cdn.ubtrobot.com/resources/image/2025/07/25/701620707123269.png).

Three task points cover stacked tote handling, automotive flow-rack sorting and assembly-kit completeness inspection. UBTECH’s issuer report explicitly names Walker S2 for these applications, but does not provide per-site accepted-cycle or intervention counts, nor inspection error rates. All three stay at manufacturer_claim. Delivery totals for the wider product family are not task-performance evidence. [Issuer results, page 10](https://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0331/2026033102607.pdf).

Walker S2 EDU documentation describes a different 42-DoF configuration and a research/teaching usage scope. EDU figures and operating permissions are excluded from the industrial record. The separately discussed fifth-generation hands are also not assumed included. [EDU scope](https://docs.ubtrobot.com/walker-s2-edu/docs/user-guide/5/).
